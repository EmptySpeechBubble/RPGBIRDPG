================================================================================
                    SEASONS OF THE FLOCK
              A Bird RPG / Seasonal Strategy Game
                      Design Document v1.0
================================================================================

CORE CONCEPT
------------
A turn-based RPG where you control a single bird navigating the challenges of 
survival across multiple seasons and years. Combines personal bird progression 
(stats, abilities, relationships) with seasonal strategic planning (territory 
management, resource gathering, flock leadership). The game leverages the 
existing ecosystem simulation but adds player agency through meaningful choices 
each turn.


GAME LOOP
---------
MACRO LOOP (Yearly Cycle):
- Spring → Summer → Fall → Winter → Repeat
- Each season = 10-15 turns
- Goal: Survive multiple years, raise successful offspring, become flock leader

MICRO LOOP (Per Turn):
1. Survey the Area (see nearby entities, resources, threats)
2. Choose Action (move, forage, build, socialize, rest, combat)
3. Resolve Action (dice rolls, skill checks, resource changes)
4. World Reacts (other birds act, predators move, weather changes)
5. Update Status (hunger, energy, season progress)
6. End Turn


PLAYER CHARACTER - THE BIRD
----------------------------
CORE STATS:
- Health: 100 max (death at 0)
- Hunger: 0-100 (lose health if reaches 100)
- Energy: 0-100 (needed for actions; sleep restores)
- Social Standing: 0-100 (affects flock interactions)

SKILLS (Level 1-10):
- Foraging: Find food more efficiently
- Building: Construct nests/birdhouses faster, better quality
- Flying: Move farther per turn, better aerial maneuvers
- Combat: Defend territory, fight predators
- Flocking: Lead/coordinate with other birds
- Perception: Spot threats, hidden resources

ATTRIBUTES (Based on Bird Type):
- Species: Bluebird, Cardinal, Canary, Robin, Sparrow
- Aggression: 0.2-0.7 (affects territorial behavior)
- Flocking Tendency: 0.5-0.95 (affects social interactions)
- Size/Speed: Multipliers for movement and combat

PROGRESSION:
- Gain XP from: surviving seasons, completing quests, successful hunts
- Level up: Increase one skill, gain 10 max health
- Max Level: 10 (Master Bird)


SEASONAL MECHANICS
------------------

=== SPRING (Renewal & Territory) ===
KEY ACTIVITIES:
- Claim territory (choose nesting area)
- Find/court a mate
- Gather nest materials (10 twigs needed)
- Build nest or birdhouse
- Lay eggs (if mated)

CHALLENGES:
- Competition for prime nesting spots
- Territorial disputes with other birds
- Material scarcity
- Predators targeting nests

SEASON-SPECIFIC MECHANICS:
- Courtship mini-game (series of successful actions to impress mate)
- Territory size affects resource access
- Can join an existing flock or start your own

QUESTS:
- "First Home": Build your first nest
- "Perfect Match": Successfully court a mate
- "Territorial Victor": Defend your territory 3 times
- "Community Builder": Help 2 other birds build nests


=== SUMMER (Growth & Protection) ===
KEY ACTIVITIES:
- Feed babies (requires 3 food per baby per turn)
- Defend nest from predators (rats, squirrels, crows)
- Forage extensively (more worms, abundant food)
- Expand territory
- Train fledglings

CHALLENGES:
- Rat/Rat King raids
- Squirrel nest invasions  
- Hunger management (feeding family)
- Weather events (storms)
- Owl attacks at night

SEASON-SPECIFIC MECHANICS:
- Baby birds grow: Egg → Hatchling → Fledgling → Independent
- Family defense: Must protect nest, combat more common
- Heat waves reduce energy faster
- Abundant food but competition increases

QUESTS:
- "Provider": Successfully feed babies for 5 turns
- "Guardian": Defeat a nest raider
- "Mentor": Train a fledgling to independence
- "Flock Leader": Coordinate flock defense against predator


=== FALL (Preparation & Decision) ===
KEY ACTIVITIES:
- Cache food for winter (hide 20+ food items)
- Fortify nest/birdhouse
- Make migration decision (stay or go?)
- Establish winter territory
- Form survival alliances

CHALLENGES:
- Dwindling food sources
- Increasingly aggressive competition
- Weather becomes harsh (wind, early frost)
- Predators desperate for food
- Migration risks vs. winter risks

SEASON-SPECIFIC MECHANICS:
- Food caching system: Hide food in trees, retrieve later
- Migration choice: Leave (easier winter, lose territory) or Stay (claim 
  abandoned areas, face hardship)
- Weather deteriorates: More rain, wind, temperature drops
- Flock dynamics shift: Some birds leave, changing social structure

QUESTS:
- "Winter Prep": Cache 20 food items
- "Fortification": Improve nest durability to 100%
- "Tough Choice": Make migration decision (stay earns bonus standing)
- "Alliance": Form winter survival pact with 3 birds


=== WINTER (Survival & Endurance) ===
KEY ACTIVITIES:
- Retrieve cached food
- Huddle for warmth (with flock)
- Seek shelter during storms
- Defend caches from thieves
- Survive until spring

CHALLENGES:
- Severe food scarcity (no worms, few seeds)
- Freezing temperatures (energy drains faster)
- Blizzards and storms (must seek shelter or lose health)
- Desperate predators
- Starvation risk
- Maintaining social bonds in hardship

SEASON-SPECIFIC MECHANICS:
- Survival mode: All stats drain faster
- Weather severity: Blizzards require sheltering (lose turns)
- Cache dependency: Cached food is primary sustenance
- Flock huddling: Group with other birds to reduce energy drain (-30% drain 
  when with 3+ birds)
- Night is much longer and more dangerous

QUESTS:
- "Survivor": Reach the end of winter alive
- "Provider": Share food with struggling flock members
- "Fortress": Successfully shelter during 3 storms
- "Winter Warrior": Defend your cache from 5 theft attempts


TURN STRUCTURE
--------------
PHASE 1: OBSERVATION
- View 3x3 grid of areas around you
- See: birds, predators, food, materials, nests, weather
- Read notifications: "A crow is approaching from the east", "You hear a rat 
  nearby"

PHASE 2: CHOOSE ACTION
Select ONE major action:

MOVEMENT ACTIONS:
- Fly (2-4 spaces based on Flying skill, costs 15 energy)
- Hop (1 space, costs 5 energy)
- Perch (find tree/structure, regain 10 energy)

RESOURCE ACTIONS:
- Forage (hunt for food, Foraging check)
- Gather Material (collect twig, Building check)
- Cache Food (hide food item for later)
- Retrieve Cache (recover hidden food)

BUILDING ACTIONS:
- Build Nest (requires 10 twigs, Building check)
- Improve Nest (increase durability, Building check)
- Build Birdhouse (requires 5 materials, extended project)

SOCIAL ACTIONS:
- Court Mate (if spring, Flocking check)
- Feed Baby (requires food, -hunger for baby)
- Socialize (improve standing with nearby birds)
- Lead Flock (give commands, requires high standing)

COMBAT ACTIONS:
- Defend Territory (challenge intruder, Combat check)
- Attack (engage predator/rival, Combat check)
- Flee (escape combat, Flying check)

REST ACTIONS:
- Sleep (restore 50 energy, vulnerable, advances time)
- Shelter (weather protection, restore 20 energy)
- Bathe (at puddle, restore 15 energy, improve social)

PHASE 3: RESOLUTION
- Roll skill check (if needed)
- Apply results (gain food, build progress, damage dealt, etc.)
- Update stats (hunger +5, energy -cost)

PHASE 4: WORLD REACTION
- Other birds take actions (simplified)
- Predators move/hunt
- Weather progresses
- Random events roll

PHASE 5: STATUS UPDATE
- Check critical conditions (starvation, exhaustion, hypothermia)
- Update season timer (turn X of 15)
- Check quest progress
- Save game state


SKILL CHECKS & DICE SYSTEM
---------------------------
When attempting most actions, roll skill checks:

FORMULA: d20 + Skill Level + Stat Modifier vs. Difficulty

DIFFICULTY CLASSES:
- Trivial: DC 5 (plentiful food, calm weather)
- Easy: DC 10 (normal foraging)
- Medium: DC 15 (building in wind, contested resources)
- Hard: DC 20 (winter foraging, aggressive predator)
- Very Hard: DC 25 (blizzard survival, Rat King combat)

CRITICAL SUCCESS (Natural 20): Double effect, no energy cost
CRITICAL FAILURE (Natural 1): Negative consequence, extra energy cost

EXAMPLES:
- Foraging in Spring: d20 + Foraging skill + 0 vs. DC 10
  Success: Find 3 food, Failure: Find 1 food
  
- Combat vs. Rat: d20 + Combat skill + Aggression*10 vs. DC 15
  Success: Drive rat away, take 0 damage
  Failure: Take 20 damage, rat escapes
  
- Building Nest: d20 + Building skill + Energy/10 vs. DC 12
  Success: Add 2 twigs to nest
  Failure: Add 1 twig, material damaged


ENCOUNTERS & EVENTS
-------------------

RANDOM EVENTS (1-in-10 chance per turn):
- Predator Appearance (Owl, Crow, Rat, Hawk)
- Weather Shift (sudden rain, wind, temperature change)
- Flock Opportunity (join coordinated hunt, group defense)
- Rare Resource (premium food, perfect twig, safe birdhouse spot)
- Social Drama (rival challenges, mate interest, flock politics)
- Natural Hazard (falling branch, snake, cat)

PREDATOR ENCOUNTERS:
Each predator has stats and behavior patterns:

RAT (Common):
- Health: 30, Attack: 10-15
- Behavior: Raids nests for eggs, steals food caches
- Combat options: Attack, Distract, Flee, Call for Help
- Defeat: Gain 50 XP, protect nest

RAT KING (Rare, Boss):
- Health: 100, Attack: 20-30
- Behavior: Leads rat swarms, targets established nests
- Requires: Multiple birds or high combat skill
- Defeat: Gain 200 XP, major reputation boost

OWL (Night Predator):
- Health: 60, Attack: 25-35
- Behavior: Hunts sleeping birds at night
- Best strategy: Shelter in birdhouse, flock huddle
- Escape: Flying check DC 20

SQUIRREL (Opportunist):
- Health: 40, Attack: 12-18
- Behavior: Steals materials, occasionally raids nests
- Combat options: Intimidate (easier), Fight, Negotiate (trade food)

CROW (Rival):
- Health: 50, Attack: 15-20
- Behavior: Territorial, may steal shiny items
- Can become: Ally (quest), Neutral, Enemy
- Social encounter possible

SOCIAL ENCOUNTERS:
- Courtship: Series of skill checks (Flying displays, resource gifts)
- Territory Dispute: Combat or Intimidation check
- Flock Recruitment: Flocking check, requires standing
- Alliance Formation: Quest chain, mutual benefits
- Mentorship: Teach fledgling, improve Flocking skill


QUEST SYSTEM
------------

MAIN STORYLINE (Multi-year arc):
Year 1: "The Fledgling" - Survive your first year, establish territory
Year 2: "The Provider" - Raise successful offspring, grow flock
Year 3: "The Elder" - Become flock leader, face major threats
Year 4+: "The Legend" - Shape the ecosystem, ultimate challenges

SEASONAL QUESTS:
- Mandatory: One per season (survival focused)
- Optional: 3-5 per season (rewards, XP, items)
- Repeatable: Daily activities (forage X food, socialize)

QUEST TYPES:
- Survival: "Survive winter with health above 50"
- Collection: "Gather 10 twigs for community birdhouse"
- Combat: "Drive away 3 predators this season"
- Social: "Achieve 80 social standing"
- Exploration: "Visit all 9 territories"
- Building: "Construct a perfect nest (100% durability)"
- Family: "Raise 2 babies to fledgling stage"

QUEST REWARDS:
- Experience Points (XP for leveling)
- Items (premium food, special materials)
- Abilities (unlock new actions)
- Relationships (mate, allies, mentees)
- Reputation (social standing boost)
- Territory perks (safe zones, resource nodes)


PROGRESSION SYSTEMS
-------------------

CHARACTER ADVANCEMENT:
Level 1 → 10 over multiple years
- Each level: +1 skill point, +10 max health, +5 energy
- Level 3: Unlock "Lead Hunt" action
- Level 5: Unlock "Mentor" action, can train other birds
- Level 7: Unlock "Inspire Flock" action (boost nearby birds)
- Level 10: Unlock "Legendary" traits (species-specific ultimate abilities)

NEST/TERRITORY PROGRESSION:
- Nest Quality: Basic → Reinforced → Fortress → Legacy
- Territory Size: Single tree → Grove → Forest sector
- Benefits: +safety, +resource access, +social standing

FLOCK LEADERSHIP:
- Follower count: 0 → 3 → 7 → 12+
- Leader abilities: Call for help, coordinate hunts, organize defense
- Responsibilities: Protect flock, settle disputes, manage resources

RELATIONSHIP PROGRESSION:
- Mate: Stranger → Interest → Courting → Bonded → Lifelong
- Offspring: Track lineage, children return as adults
- Rivals: Enemy → Neutral → Respectful → Allied
- Mentor/Student: Build legacy through teaching


WIN CONDITIONS
--------------

PRIMARY WIN (Complete Campaign):
- Survive 4 complete years (16 seasons)
- Raise at least 6 offspring to independence
- Achieve "Elder" status (Level 7+, Social Standing 80+)
- Establish a legacy (thriving flock, protected territory)

ALTERNATIVE WINS:
- "Master Survivor": Survive 10 years
- "Dynasty Builder": 15+ descendants in ecosystem
- "Flock Leader": Lead 20+ birds
- "Apex Bird": Defeat all boss predators (Rat King, Hawk, Owl Alpha)
- "Architect": Build 5 birdhouses, 10 nests

SCORE SYSTEM:
- Years Survived × 1000
- Offspring Raised × 500
- Predators Defeated × 200
- Quests Completed × 100
- Final Social Standing × 10
- Skill Levels × 50


LOSE CONDITIONS
---------------
IMMEDIATE GAME OVER:
- Health reaches 0 (death)
- Fail to survive winter (starvation/freezing)
- Killed by predator

GRADUAL FAILURE:
- Hunger reaches 100 for 3 consecutive turns → Death
- Energy depleted during critical moment (fleeing, sheltering)
- Abandoned by mate after repeated failures


UNIQUE MECHANICS LEVERAGING SIMULATION
---------------------------------------

FLOCKING DYNAMICS:
- Coordinate with AI birds based on their flocking tendency
- Sparrows flock easily (easier to lead, less combat power)
- Cardinals resist flocking (harder to recruit, strong fighters)

TERRITORIAL SYSTEM:
- Aggression stat affects territorial interactions
- High-aggression species (Cardinals) defend larger areas
- Low-aggression species (Canaries) share space more easily

WEATHER INTEGRATION:
- Rain: Harder foraging, fewer flying options, puddles for bathing
- Wind: Flight checks harder, twigs blow around (easier gathering)
- Storms: Must seek shelter, lose turns, danger increases
- Snow/Ice: Winter survival mechanic, cached food essential

REALISTIC BEHAVIORS:
- Birds remember food locations (you can revisit productive spots)
- Predators have patrol patterns (learn and avoid)
- Babies grow in real-time (must commit turns to feeding)
- Nests deteriorate (maintenance required)

DAY/NIGHT CYCLE:
- Daytime: Full action set, safer, better visibility
- Nighttime: Limited actions, predators active, must rest
- Twilight: Transition period, roosting time

FOOD CHAIN:
- Seeds/berries: Easy, low energy
- Worms: Medium, good energy, appear after rain
- Insects: Hard, high energy, summer only
- Cached food: Reliable but finite

SPECIAL EVENTS FROM SIM:
- Mushroom Mode: Rare event, distorts perception, surreal encounters
- Ant Swarm: Gather lots of food quickly
- Butterfly Migration: Peaceful moment, social bonus
- Scarecrow Presence: Safe zone from ground predators

TECHNICAL NOTES FOR IMPLEMENTATION
-----------------------------------

1. Convert real-time movement to grid-based (9 territories)
2. Turn-based action queue instead of continuous updates
3. Simplify flocking to "nearby allies" modifier
4. Discrete skill checks replace probabilistic behaviors
5. Turn counter instead of time-of-day timer

PRESERVE FROM SIMULATION:
- All entity types and their behaviors
- Weather system (adapted to turn-based)
- Food/material mechanics
- Nest building system
- Predator AI patterns (simplified)
- Day/night cycle (abstract)
